# AMIS-asfi

## AMIS Sadaqah Family Incorporated (ASFI)
Official Web Platform for **AMIS Sadaqah Family Incorporated (ASFI)** — Davao City, Philippines (2026).

ASFI is the official charitable, humanitarian, and community development arm of Al Munawwara Islamic School (AMIS).

### Core Features
- **Sadaqah & Zakat Information**: Educational grants, orphan care stpend, food drives, and calamity relief.
- **Interactive Sadaqah Calculator & Payment Portal**: Easy 1-click GCash, Maya, and Bank Transfer donation details with automatic clipboard copy.
- **Transparency & Accountability**: 100% stewardship tracking and audit reporting.
- **Volunteer & Partner Portal**: Community engagement and partnership sign-ups.

### Production Domain
- `https://asfi.amis.edu.ph`
